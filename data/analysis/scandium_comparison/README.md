The manuscript electronic comparison uses seven Sc-centered and three Co-centered clean/H pairs. Sc–Mn–Cr–Fe is excluded from this comparison because its H-state static calculation changes the relaxed magnetic state; its adsorption result remains among the eight Sc validation compositions.

The H-state mean occupied d-state centroids are −3.68 eV for Sc and −2.74 eV for Co relative to their respective Fermi levels. Mean H Bader populations are 1.636 and 1.058 electrons; mean metal–H distances are 1.866 and 1.447 Å. These charge and geometry results support stronger ionic character for Sc.

Electronic result tables are under [data/processed/electronic_structure](../../processed/electronic_structure/). Definitions and complete per-state values are in [SI Section S1.8 and Tables S13–S14](../../../docs/methods/supporting_information_EN.md#s1-8).
