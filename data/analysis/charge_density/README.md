# Remote-site charge responses

[bader_charges.csv](bader_charges.csv) and [bader_charge_analysis.txt](bader_charge_analysis.txt) give Bader electron populations for clean and H-adsorbed structures in three remote-site exchange pairs. In these files, `ΔQ(H-clean)` is the change in electron population; positive values mean electron gain.

The six differences between the two remote-site responses are 0.0092, 0.0000, 0.0065, 0.0002, 0.0307 and 0.0382 electrons, in the order listed in the text table. Four are below 0.01 electrons.

Electronic and geometry result tables are under [data/processed/electronic_structure](../../processed/electronic_structure/). Co–N₄ net-charge definitions and bonding comparisons are described in the [electronic-structure guide](../electronic_structure/README.md).
